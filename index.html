Questions buyers ask

Before you book

What does the diagnostic cost?

A fixed fee between $45k and $90k depending on the number of sites and business units. We quote it in writing after the first call, before any work starts.

How much of our team's time will this take?

Roughly two hours a week from the sponsor, a half-day from each function head during interviews, and one data owner for the ERP extract. We do the analysis.

We already have a consultancy. Why you?

Our partners have run P&Ls, and they do the work themselves. We are paid on a plan you can audit, and the fee-back guarantee means we carry the risk on the diagnostic.

What if we only want the diagnostic?

That's fine. Around a third of clients run the plan themselves. You own every model and playbook we produce.

How do you handle confidential data?

We sign your NDA or ours before any data request. Data stays in your tenant or an encrypted workspace and is deleted at the end of the engagement.
